# Maple Street Scoops — shop information

Maple Street Scoops is a FICTIONAL neighborhood ice cream shop used for this evaluation.
Everything below is invented. It is the only knowledge the assistant is given.

## Location and contact
- Address: 214 Maple Street, Riverbend, Indiana
- Phone: (812) 555-0142
- Email: hello@maplestreetscoops.example
- Parking: free lot behind the building, plus street parking on Maple Street.

## Hours
- Monday: closed
- Tuesday–Thursday: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM
- Friday–Saturday: 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM
- Sunday: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Open year-round. The walk-up window closes at the same time as the shop.

## Payment
- Cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- No personal checks.

## Menu and prices
Scoops (cup, or a cake or sugar cone at no charge):
- Kids scoop (ages 12 and under): $3.00
- 1 scoop: $4.25
- 2 scoops: $6.25
- 3 scoops: $8.00
- Waffle cone: add $1.25. Chocolate-dipped waffle cone: add $1.75.
- Toppings: $0.75 each (sprinkles, crushed Oreo, hot fudge, caramel, gummy bears). Whipped cream is free.

Shakes and sundaes:
- Milkshake (16 oz, any flavor): $6.95. Make it a malt: add $0.50.
- Hot fudge sundae (2 scoops, hot fudge, whipped cream, cherry): $7.50
- Banana split (3 scoops, banana, three toppings, whipped cream): $8.95

Take-home:
- Hand-packed pint: $8.50
- Hand-packed quart: $14.00

## Flavors (current lineup)
Vanilla Bean, Chocolate, Strawberry, Butter Pecan, Mint Chocolate Chip, Cookie Dough,
Black Raspberry, Coffee Toffee, Salted Caramel, Cookies & Cream, Peanut Butter Swirl,
Birthday Cake, Rocky Road, Lemon Sorbet, Mango Sorbet, and a rotating seasonal flavor
(Pumpkin Pie in the fall).

Lemon Sorbet and Mango Sorbet are made without milk or cream.

## Ordering
- Online ordering on our website is for **pickup only**. We do not deliver.
- Pints and quarts can be ordered online for pickup.

## Loyalty
- Free punch card: buy 9 scoops, the 10th scoop is free. Ask at the register.

## Gift cards
- Sold in the shop only, in any amount from $10 to $100. Not sold online.

## Catering
- We bring an ice cream cart to events of 50 or more guests.
- Book at least two weeks ahead by calling the shop.

## Pets
- Dogs are welcome on the patio, and every dog gets a free pup cup.
- Only service animals are allowed inside.

## Seating
- About 20 seats inside, plus a patio that is open in warm weather.
